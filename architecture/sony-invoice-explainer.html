Three integration alternatives

ApproachWhen it fitsConsequence
A · Reuse VIM (preferred)Required intake, status and business-action interfaces exist or can be exposed.New case and AI layer assists the existing business process.
B · Limited integrationOnly some VIM operations are accessible.Automate supported steps; users apply recommendations in VIM. Retain existing intake if necessary.
C · Custom Sony processReuse is unsuitable or Sony elects broader replacement.Sony owns replacement checks, routing, coding and approval; SAP still executes accounting.

Three stories to discuss

  1. Non-PO + Excel: preserve the evidence, resolve a captured-data error, propose a supported allocation, obtain coder and approver decisions, confirm posting.
  2. PO matching: reuse PO account assignment, map ambiguous invoice lines, validate against SAP context and route genuine discrepancies.
  3. Late supporting document: retain a waiting case, associate the later email, revalidate affected information and resume the existing process.

Assumptions to retain

S/4HANA is the target ERP. Sony’s VIM version and usable integration contracts are unconfirmed. New Joule Studio/n8n availability is a deployment dependency. Extraction results require validation. Supporting files can be shared across cases, with versioned associations. Human approval and posting order follow the applicable business process.

Document AI performs extraction; the surrounding intake service preserves email/file relationships. Joule Studio’s SAP-managed runtime is distinct from the Cloud Foundry/Kyma deployment of custom extensions. Payment runs and post-posting RTR reallocations remain outside scope.
